About Lake Brew

A cosy place for chai, food and good conversations.

Our story

Good drinks. Better moments.

Lake Brew is a lakeview café in Kohefiza, Bhopal, built around a simple idea: create a comfortable place where people can enjoy a great drink without feeling rushed.

From a quiet solo chai to an evening catch-up with friends, our space is made for everyday moments worth remembering.

  • Relaxed lakeview setting
  • Chai, coffee and café favourites
  • Friendly space for everyone
